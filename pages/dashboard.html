    About the Study

    SKYSHIELD AI: An Intelligent Weather Risk Prediction and Personalized Preparedness System for Students

    Background of the Study

    Weather conditions can greatly affect students' daily activities, especially when travelling to and from school. Heavy rainfall may cause flooding, traffic congestion and transportation delays, while thunderstorms may create dangerous travel conditions. Extreme heat can lead to discomfort, dehydration and other heat-related health concerns. These situations may affect students' safety, attendance and ability to participate in school activities.

    Weather forecasts provide general information, but they may not give students specific recommendations based on their daily activities and travel needs. SKYSHIELD AI analyzes available weather information, identifies possible risks, classifies them as low, moderate or high, and gives understandable alerts and personalized preparedness recommendations.

    It is an additional preparedness tool and does not replace official weather advisories or emergency instructions.

    Statement of the Problem

    Students may encounter weather-related risks such as heavy rainfall, thunderstorms and extreme heat while travelling to and from school. Although general forecasts are available, students still need clear risk assessments and practical recommendations. This study seeks to answer:

    1. How can SKYSHIELD AI analyze available weather data to identify potential risks associated with heavy rainfall, thunderstorms and extreme heat?
    2. How can the system classify weather-related risks into low, moderate and high levels?
    3. How can the system provide personalized weather alerts and preparedness recommendations based on conditions and risk levels?
    4. How can SKYSHIELD AI help students improve their awareness and preparedness before travelling to school?
    5. How can the functionality, reliability and usability of SKYSHIELD AI be evaluated?
    General Objective

    To develop SKYSHIELD AI, an intelligent weather risk prediction and personalized preparedness system designed to help students identify potential weather-related risks and receive appropriate preparedness recommendations. The system aims to improve students' awareness, readiness and ability to respond to different weather conditions through AI-assisted weather risk analysis and personalized safety information.

    Coverage

    SKYSHIELD AI uses available weather information and forecasts to identify potential hazards and provide safety recommendations. It focuses on three major conditions: heavy rainfall, thunderstorms and extreme heat, helping students prepare before travelling to school. The study also evaluates the system's functionality, reliability and usability.
