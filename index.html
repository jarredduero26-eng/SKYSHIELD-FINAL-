About the Study

Weather affects students' safety, attendance and travel. General forecasts rarely say what to prepare, so SkyShield AI turns weather conditions into clear risk levels and personal recommendations.

  • Analyze rain, thunderstorm and heat risks
  • Classify risks as low, moderate or high
  • Give personalized alerts and preparation tips
  • Improve awareness before travelling to school

Students, teachers, school administrators, parents and guardians, and future researchers.

  • This offline version uses the weather values you enter.
  • It does not download live weather forecasts.
  • Weather information may not always be completely accurate.
  • It does not replace official PAGASA or local advisories.

MODERATE

Prepare the items relevant to your trip.

HIGH
70 - 100

Review your travel plan and prepare carefully.
